Classes: triacylglycerols (glycerol + 3 fatty acids; saponifiable) · glycerophospholipids (glycerol + 2 fatty acids + phosphate + alcohol such as choline) · sphingolipids (sphingosine + 1 fatty acid by an amide + phosphate/choline or a sugar) · steroids (four fused rings: cholesterol, bile salts, hormones, vitamin D) · waxes (fatty acid + long alcohol ester) · eicosanoids (prostaglandins, from arachidonic acid). Lipoproteins by rising density: chylomicron → VLDL → LDL → HDL.
